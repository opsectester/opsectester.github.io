**Unpacked** is where I write down what I find when I take malware and phishing apart. Repackaged apps, phishing kits, packers, the odd APK that turns out to be more than it claims — if it's interesting and I can pull it to pieces, it ends up here.

Everything is independent research. Samples are handled on an isolated analysis bench — REMnux in a container, all traffic forced through a kill-switched VPN, nothing ever run on real hardware or a real phone. If a post says I detonated something, it happened in a sandbox and stayed there.

I publish indicators in full and un-redacted. Defanged for click-safety, sure, but never held back. A hash or a domain that stays in my notes helps nobody; the whole point is that someone else chasing the same thing can pivot straight off it. Where a campaign is live, I report it to the relevant parties — ReportCyber, the hosting provider, the registrar — before or alongside publishing, not instead of it.

I'll also tell you where I got stuck. Not every case ends with the C2 in hand. "Here's exactly how far I got and the wall I hit" is more honest, and usually more useful, than a tidy story that skips the hard part.

Nothing here is affiliated with any employer or client, and nothing here is advice — it's a lab notebook I happen to make public.

Seen the same sample, or cracked something I couldn't? The GitHub and email links are in the sidebar. I'd genuinely like to compare notes.
